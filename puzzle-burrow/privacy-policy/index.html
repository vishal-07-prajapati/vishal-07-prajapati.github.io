Privacy Policy for Puzzle Burrow

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Developer: Bright Pattern Games

Contact: support@brightpatterngames.com

Puzzle Burrow ("the app") is a jigsaw puzzle game for young children, made by Bright Pattern Games ("we"). We built it so that it does not collect any information about the children who play it or the grown-ups who look after them.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no way for a child to type in their name or any other details. We do not run our own servers and we have no analytics or tracking tools.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a short full-screen ad between puzzles now and then. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a Google Play Families self-certified ads SDK, and are set up for children:

  • Every ad request is marked as directed to children (COPPA) and under the age of consent (GDPR), and ads are limited to G-rated content.
  • Ads are never personalised. No interest-based or remarketing ads are shown.
  • The app does not use the Android advertising ID. We remove the advertising-ID and ad-personalisation permissions from the app.
  • Ads appear only between puzzles, never during play, never when the app opens, and not during a child's first puzzles.

To deliver ads, measure that they were shown and prevent fraud, AdMob receives limited technical information from the device, such as the IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device type, operating system, app version, crash and diagnostic information, an app-specific identifier (the Android app set ID, which is not the advertising ID) and how the ad was interacted with. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and Google's policies for child-directed ad requests. We do not receive any information that identifies your child.

3. Remove ads (one-time purchase)

A grown-up can remove all ads with a one-time purchase. It is only offered inside the grown-ups area, behind a parental gate. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether this purchase has been made, so ads stay removed after reinstalling.

4. Internet use

The app uses the internet only to load ads and to check the "Remove ads" purchase with Google Play. Puzzles work fully offline.

5. Device permissions

The app does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.

6. Information stored on the device

To remember a child's progress, the app saves the following only on the device itself:

  • which puzzles have been finished, the stars earned, and the best number of mistakes and time for each,
  • whether the first-time tutorial has been seen,
  • settings chosen in the grown-ups area (sound, music, picture guide, play-time reminder, reduced motion).

This information never leaves the device through the app. It is deleted when you tap Reset Data in the grown-ups area or uninstall the app. If Android's own device backup is turned on, Android may include this game data in your personal Google account backup; that backup is controlled by you and Google, and we cannot see it.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Game data on the device (progress and settings, section 6) is kept until you tap Reset Data in the grown-ups area or uninstall the app. We have no copy of it.
  • We keep no data about players. We do not run servers, and we do not store information about anyone who plays the game.
  • Information Google AdMob receives to deliver ads (section 2) goes directly to Google. We never receive it, so we cannot view or delete it. Google keeps and deletes it according to the Google Privacy Policy and how Google retains data.
  • The "Remove ads" purchase is recorded by Google Play in the buyer's Google account and is managed there.
  • Questions about data: email us at the address in section 11.

8. Grown-ups area

Settings and the "Remove ads" purchase are behind a parental gate (a multiplication question written in words) so that young children cannot reach them.

9. Children's privacy laws

We do not collect personal information from children. Ad requests are flagged for child-directed treatment so that Google handles them under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and as under the age of consent for the EU/UK GDPR. The app is built to meet the Google Play Families Policy.

10. Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date before the change takes effect. We will never start collecting personal information from children without first getting verifiable parental consent where the law requires it.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy or the app: support@brightpatterngames.com
